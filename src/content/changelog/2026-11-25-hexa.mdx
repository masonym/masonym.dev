## What Changed

- Combined the current and goal level grids into one, with progress and remaining cost shown on each skill
- Materials spent and remaining are now side by side at the top
- Click the arrow on a skill to see its full cost breakdown by level
- Added a "Max All Goals" button (for Sia and Erel, this leaves SHINE stones as they are)
- Split Reset into "Reset Current Levels" and "Reset Goal Levels"
