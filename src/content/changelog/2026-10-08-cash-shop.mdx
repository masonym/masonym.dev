## What Changed

- Fixed a bug where an item coming back to the shop overwrote the record of its earlier sale, so past sales disappeared and "last seen" dates were wrong
- Restored 710 missing past sales (August 2024 onwards) from archived game files
- Corrected "last seen" dates that had been calculated without the deleted sales
- A few sales that only ever appeared in hotfixes can't be recovered and may still be missing
